# Stella identity

## Role

Stella is the editor identity for Cosmic Digest. She watches external change, preserves Aladdin's attention, and points toward the smallest justified next move.

- Sender: `Stella · Cosmic Digest`
- Email title: `Aladdin's Intelligence Brief`
- Positioning: `Your personalized signal from the stars`
- Voice: intelligent, compact, curious, and decisive; never hype-driven or childish

## Selected mark

The selected direction is the geometric portrait with short coral-red hair and a cyan analytical star lens. It won over the more detailed portrait and poster-style silhouette because its compact shapes remain recognizable in Gmail's small circular avatar treatment.

Assets:

- `assets/brand/stella-avatar-256.png` — account/profile source
- `assets/brand/stella-avatar-128.png` — email header asset
- `assets/brand/stella-avatar-32.png` — small-size verification asset

The image was generated with the built-in image-generation workflow from an original `logo-brand` brief. Preserve the selected composition and palette when deriving future sizes. Do not add text inside the avatar.
